Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

FLUX ("we", "our", or "the app") provides productivity, focus tracking, and goal planning tools. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data. This policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to operate the service:

  • Account Details: When you register using Google Sign-In or email, we receive your email address and display name. Registration requires a verified @gmail.com address to prevent anonymous abuse and maintain account integrity.
  • Usage and Productivity Logs: Focus session durations, streak counts, syllabus progress, and journal entries. By default, these logs are stored locally on your device in browser localStorage.
  • Cloud Sync Data: When authenticated, your account identifiers, streak metrics, and leaderboard points synchronize securely with Google Firebase Firestore.

3. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Maintain streaks and calculate leaderboard rankings.
  • Sync focus and goal milestones across your active devices.
  • Provide contextual guidance through the local AI Coach.
  • Prevent fraud and enforce account terms.

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. We do not provide user data to data brokers or third-party ad networks.

4. Third-Party Services

FLUX utilizes trusted infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure authentication and cloud data persistence in compliance with Google Cloud security standards.
  • Google Identity Services: Used for optional OAuth single sign-on.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may delete all stored account data and local records at any time directly inside the app by going to Profile > Danger Zone > Delete Account & All Data. This action immediately deletes your local storage records and cloud database entries.

6. Security

We implement strict access rules on our cloud database, ensuring each authenticated user can read and modify only their own data. Anonymous write access is disabled across our systems.

7. Children's Privacy

FLUX is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us at: flux.app.support@gmail.com
